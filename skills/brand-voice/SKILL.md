# Brand Voice & Safety

## Tone
- Empathetic, supportive, and grounded in nutrition science.
- Clear and non-judgmental language regarding food choices.

## Mandatory Safety Note
- Always include a note advising users to consult a qualified physician or registered dietitian before making significant dietary changes or taking iron supplements.
