# Diet Planner Rules

- Estimate recommended daily iron intake based on age, gender, and dietary style.
- Plant-based diets require ~1.8x higher iron targets due to non-heme bioavailability.
- On vegetarian days, always pair non-heme iron (lentils, spinach, tofu) with Vitamin C (citrus, bell peppers).
- Create structured 7-day meal plans for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.
