# Grocery List Specialist Rules

- Group items strictly by department: 🥦 Produce, 🌾 Pantry & Grains, 🥩 Proteins & Meat, 🥛 Dairy & Refrigerated.
- Consolidate repeated ingredients across days into total quantities.
- Highlight high-iron ingredients with a 🩺 icon.
